$ cargo run -p rdm --example write_map -- --help
write_map: Making a starter map
Hosted models write the house's routines once, and the map is saved.

--routines   Routines to have written, one per line
    Good night: turn the TV and the lights off, close the door and lock it, and bring the heat down to 18 degrees.
    Movie time: turn the TV on and the lights off.
    Good morning: turn the lights on, unlock the door, and bring the heat up to 21 degrees.
    Given once for each line; it starts as these.

--house   Where the house starts
    JSON; it starts as {"tv": "on", "lights": "on", "door": "open", "lock": "unlocked", "heat": 21}

--to   Where to save the map, where it is not starter_map.json in the temporary directory

--trace   Show the run as the site does: every request, under the one whose tool made it (or RDM_TRACE=1).

An input can also be given without its name, in the order above:
    cargo run -p rdm --example write_map -- "..."

The models are chosen by the environment (crates/rdm/examples/README.md):
    RDM_DECISION_MODEL   standin | typesafe | openrouter | local | <url> | gateway:<registry.json> | lm
    RDM_LANGUAGE_MODEL   standin | <an lm15 model, as openrouter:typesafe/jev-router> | <url>
With neither set and no key in the environment, both are stand-ins and nothing leaves this machine.
