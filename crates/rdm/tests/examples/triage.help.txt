$ cargo run -p rdm --example triage -- --help
triage: One request
State in, typed decisions out. A yes calls the tool of the same name, with an argument the model chose.

--message   A message from a customer
    1  Charged twice: I was charged twice for the same order and nobody answers my emails. I want my money back now.
    2  Logged out: The app logs me out every time I open the settings page. Otherwise I love it!
    3  Team pricing: Could you tell me what the team plan costs for twelve people?
    4  Thanks: Just wanted to say thanks, the new export is great.
    A number picks one of these, and anything else is the text itself. It starts as 1.

--trace   Show the run as the site does: every request, under the one whose tool made it (or RDM_TRACE=1).

An input can also be given without its name, in the order above:
    cargo run -p rdm --example triage -- "..."

The models are chosen by the environment (crates/rdm/examples/README.md):
    RDM_DECISION_MODEL   standin | typesafe | openrouter | local | <url> | gateway:<registry.json> | lm
    RDM_LANGUAGE_MODEL   standin | <an lm15 model, as openrouter:typesafe/jev-router> | <url>
With neither set and no key in the environment, both are stand-ins and nothing leaves this machine.
