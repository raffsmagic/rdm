# Time off

Time away from work is recorded in the staff portal before it starts.

## Annual leave

Full-time staff receive 25 days of annual leave each calendar year. Up to five unused days carry over into the following year and must be taken by the end of March.

Leave of more than ten consecutive working days needs approval from a department head at least six weeks ahead.

## Sick leave

Tell your manager before 9:30 on the first day you are unwell. A doctor's note is required from the fourth consecutive day of absence.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks at full pay. Secondary caregivers receive six weeks at full pay, taken within the first year.

# Expenses

Claims are submitted within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemized receipt.

## Travel

Book travel through the company portal so that it is covered by the group insurance.

### Flights

Economy class applies to flights under six hours. Premium economy may be booked for longer flights.

### Hotels

The nightly limit for accommodation is 180 euros in most cities and 260 euros in London, Zurich and New York. Breakfast is included in that limit.

### Ground transport

Use public transport where it is practical. Taxis are reimbursed when travelling with equipment or after 22:00.

## Meals

When travelling, meals are reimbursed up to 45 euros per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Equipment

Each employee may claim up to 400 euros every two years for home office furniture. Monitors and keyboards are ordered through the IT catalogue instead and are not claimed as expenses.

# Security

## Passwords

Passwords are at least 14 characters and are stored in the company password manager. Two-factor authentication is mandatory for every account that supports it.

## Devices

Laptops lock after five minutes of inactivity and have disk encryption turned on. Personal phones may access email only after enrolling in device management.

## Reporting an incident

Report a lost device or a suspected breach to the security desk within one hour, at any time of day. Do not attempt to investigate it yourself.

# Working remotely

## Eligibility

Staff may work remotely up to three days a week after their probation period ends. Fully remote arrangements need director approval.

## Working from abroad

Working from another country is limited to 20 working days a year because of tax and social security rules, and needs approval from the people team before travel is booked.
